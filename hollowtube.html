HollowTube

The Organic Breathy Resonator

"Inject organic, physical breath and acoustic resonant spaces into static digital audio."

What is HollowTube?

HollowTube is an advanced physical modeling audio effect designed to transform dry synths, static percussions, and sterile recordings into living, breathing acoustic spaces.

Unlike conventional static EQs or basic comb filters, HollowTube solves the 1D wave equation in real-time using a digital waveguide resonator loop. It accurately simulates how sound waves propagate, reflect, and dissipate inside an enclosed acoustic tube or human vocal tract—generating rich odd-harmonic formants reminiscent of blown bottles, woodwinds, and deep throat resonances.

Key Features

  • True 1D Waveguide Physical Modeling: Simulates wave propagation and boundary reflections in real-time with continuous physical dissipation.
  • 1st-Order All-Pass Fractional Delay: Zero phase-distortion and pitch drift across the entire frequency range (40 Hz to 800 Hz), eliminating standard discrete sampling pitch errors.
  • Perceptually Calibrated Controls:
    • Cutoff (x): Adaptive SVF-TPT High-Pass filter (20 Hz - 2000 Hz) ensuring low-end energy stays tight before entering the feedback loop.
    • Wet Mix (a): Smooth parallel processing balance (0% - 100%).
    • Hollow Decay (b): Logarithmically calibrated T60 decay time (0.02 s - 8.0 s) derived via inverse reflection equations for smooth, natural knob response.
    • Formant Pitch (c): Precise fundamental resonance tuning (40 Hz - 800 Hz).
  • Vector-Glow GUI: Clean, vector-rendered interface with real-time travelling wave visualization and custom matte grain background.
  • Ultra-Lightweight & Zero Latency: Written in C++ / JUCE with SIMD-optimized processing for high-instance DAW usage.

Perfect For

  1. Acoustic Texture Injection: Convert simple synth plucks into woodwind-like acoustic instruments.
  2. Snare & Percussion Body: Add low-mid T60 hollow resonance to give drums a unique physical room signature.
  3. Ambient & Drone Generation: Extend Hollow Decay to create infinite, evolving vocal pad textures.
  4. Vocal Formant Shifting: Apply to vocal buses for eerie, hollow nasal or throat resonance effects.
