RT Harshness Tamer

Musical Harmonic Suppressor

Introduction

RT Harshness Tamer is a musical harmonic processor plugin that tames harsh, piercing overtones — without ever making your sound feel hollow, sterile, or "de-essed to death."

Most harshness/de-essing tools just cut. RT Harshness Tamer listens first: it detects your source's fundamental frequency in real time, locates the 2nd harmonic (the frequency most responsible for that piercing "edge"), and gently tames it — while simultaneously reinforcing the odd harmonics (3rd, 5th) that give a sound its warmth and body. The result is a mix that sits smoother in the room without losing character.

Why It Sounds Different

Built on a philosophy of "musical, not clinical" DSP:

  • Soft suppression, never hard-zero: the 2nd harmonic is dialed down, never deleted, so timbre stays natural instead of feeling gutted.
  • Energy redistribution: what's taken from the harsh harmonic is routed to the warm odd harmonics, so nothing just "disappears."
  • Phase-coherent reconstruction: 75%-overlap Hann windowing with proper COLA-normalized overlap-add means zero digital "swoosh" or aliasing artifacts, even on revealing headphones.
  • Adjustable analysis resolution ("Stage Width"): from tight and responsive to ultra-precise, so you can match the tool to fast transients or long sustained material.

Controls

  • STAGE WIDTH: sets analysis resolution, from Intimate (fast, tight) up to Infinity (maximum precision, ideal for mastering/sustained sources).
  • EDGE TAME: how strongly the 2nd harmonic is suppressed (0 to -60 dB). Never fully removes it — always keeps a natural floor.
  • WARMTH BLEND: redirects the energy taken from the 2nd harmonic into the 3rd and 5th harmonics, keeping the sound full and warm instead of hollow.
  • SILK: time-domain naturalization strength; smooths any remaining digital edge for a more organic result.
  • PRESENCE MIX: dry/wet blend, perfect for parallel-processing style workflows.
  • ORBIT REVEAL: choose how the visualizer animates (Flick or Waterdrop) — pure eye-candy, zero effect on audio.

Overtone Orbit Visualizer

Watch your harmonics live in an orbital display: the fundamental, the tamed 2nd harmonic being "pulled inward," and the 3rd/5th harmonics glowing warmer as Warmth Blend increases. It's not just a meter — it's a real-time picture of exactly what the plugin is doing to your sound.
